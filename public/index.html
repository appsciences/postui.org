AI is the new UI

A working group for moving software from application-centric interfaces to assistant-centric ones. We take real products apart in public and rebuild them as intents, tools and resources — then we build the pilots and write down what held.

> cat manifesto.md
the argument, in ten theses

Most software is CRUD plus business rules. The screens were never the product — they were the only way we had to reach the data and the rules behind them. That constraint is gone.

An assistant collapses navigation into intent. The seven-screen workflow becomes one sentence and a tool call. What survives as interface is the part that was always visual — comparison, spatial data, ambient state, anything with real bandwidth. The rest was scaffolding for a database we can now simply ask.

> cat teardown.template
every teardown uses the same nine fields. that is what makes a pile of posts into a canon.
# teardown/<product>.md

product      the thing as it ships today
surfaces     every screen, tab, form and modal the user must traverse
job          what they were actually trying to do — never "use the app"
intents      the three to seven things anyone ever asks for
state        what must persist between asks — preferences, history, identity
tools        actions the assistant must be able to take
resources    data it must be able to read
stays_gui    what genuinely needs pixels, and the reason why
breaks       where this decomposition falls apart. required field.
see a worked example→ breaks is mandatory — a teardown with no failure mode is marketing
